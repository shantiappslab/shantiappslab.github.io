Wild mushroom guide – Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Developer: shantiappslab · Contact: shantiappslab@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the Android app published by shantiappslab as a wild mushroom guide for each country (for example «Pilze Deutschland», «Setas de Andorra» or «Mushrooms of the US»; «the app», package com.shantiappslab.mushroomsofeurope) uses, and why.

1. Information we collect

We have no servers of our own, the app has no user accounts and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information (see the Google services in section 2). All the content (mushroom guides, recipes, picking rules, maps and game) is inside the app and works offline.

The following data is stored only on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app: the language and country you choose, game records and medals, unlocked recipes, the date you accepted the initial notice and whether you have the Premium version.

Mushroom notebook (Premium feature): if you ask it to, the app saves the place where you found a mushroom (using your phone's GPS, with your permission), a photo taken with the camera or chosen from your gallery, and your notes. This data is stored only on your device: it is not sent anywhere, we never see it and it is deleted if you delete the entry or uninstall the app. If you agree, the app can show a local reminder («One year ago…») a year after an outing; it is calculated on the phone, without sending anything. You can also export the notebook to a file you choose. The app does not use your contacts or any other sensitive permission.

2. Third-party services

The app uses these Google services, which may collect information under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (ads between screens and optional rewarded ads): may collect the device advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions in order to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. In US states with privacy laws (such as California) you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for personalised ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics): collects anonymous usage data such as the screens and guides viewed, games played, session length, ads seen, approximate location (country), device information and an app installation identifier, to understand how the app is used and improve it. It does not identify you personally. In the EEA this information is only stored if you give your consent.
  • Google Play Billing (the optional «Premium» purchase): payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; we only know whether the purchase was made.
  • Google Play: distributes the app and may collect installation and crash information.
  • Weather data (rain, temperature and mushroom probability maps): the app downloads a file with data summarised by region, prepared from the open data of ECMWF and DWD (Deutscher Wetterdienst) and published on our website on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com / shantiappslab.github.io). No personal data or location is sent; as with any web request, the GitHub server receives the IP address of the connection.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. Your choices

  • You can change your ad consent at any time from Info → Privacy options in the app (shown where it is required in your region).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: they are only shown when you decide to watch one.
  • The «Premium» purchase removes all ads from the app.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your device.

4. United States (California and other states)

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for our own purposes. The only personal information processed is the one handled by the Google services in section 2 (mainly AdMob for ads). Where state law gives you the right (for example the California Consumer Privacy Act, CCPA/CPRA), the app shows Google's message with the option to opt out of the sale or sharing of your information for personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time from Info → Privacy options. Data stored only on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. For any request, write to the contact address below.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Ads are limited to content suitable for all audiences.

6. Content notice

The app is an educational guide. Never eat a mushroom identified only with this app. If in any doubt, ask an expert or a mycological society. In case of poisoning, call 112 or your local emergency number (in the United States and Canada, 911; US Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222; Canada: 1-844-764-7669).
