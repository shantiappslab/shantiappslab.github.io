Without a car

  • Bus: Coach T01 Zaragoza – Huesca – Sabiñánigo – Formigal · from Zaragoza · 210 min
    Aragon public network (line T01). Stops at Huesca, Sabiñánigo, Biescas, Panticosa and Sallent. ›
