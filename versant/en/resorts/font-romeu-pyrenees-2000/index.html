How to get to Font-Romeu Pyrénées 2000

By road, right now

  • N116 Prades–Mont-Louis–Bourg-Madame · N116: No incidents
  • D618 Mont-Louis–Font-Romeu · D618: No open data

Official incidents from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé.

Without a car

  • Train: Yellow Train to Font-Romeu-Odeillo-Via or Bolquère-Eyne · from Villefranche-de-Conflent / Latour-de-Carol
    From Barcelona: R3 to Latour-de-Carol, then the Yellow Train. Font-Romeu station is 3 km from the centre; Bolquère-Eyne is close to Pyrénées 2000. ›
  • Bus: liO bus 560 Perpignan – Font-Romeu · from Perpignan
    Several journeys a day from Perpignan station to the centre of Font-Romeu. ›
  • Shuttle: Font-Romeu ski bus · from Font-Romeu (village)
    There is also a gondola from the village centre to the foot of the slopes (Les Airelles). ›
